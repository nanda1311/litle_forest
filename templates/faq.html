Frequently asked questions

  • What is a bonsai plant?

    A bonsai is a miniature tree grown in a pot, shaped and trained over time using specific techniques. It reflects the beauty of a full-sized tree in a compact, artistic form.

  • What are some common bonsai species in India?

    Popular bonsai species in India include Ficus, Banyan, Jade, Neem, Peepal, Chinese Elm, and Juniper. These adapt well to Indian weather and are ideal for beginners and hobbyists alike.

  • Can bonsais be grown indoors?

    Yes, many bonsai trees like Ficus and Jade do well indoors. Just ensure they get enough indirect sunlight and are placed in a well-ventilated area.

  • How do I take care of bonsais?

    Bonsai care involves proper watering, pruning, sunlight, and occasional repotting. Each species has different needs, so it's important to understand your tree’s specific requirements.

  • How often should I water my bonsai?

    Watering depends on the species, pot size, and weather. As a general rule, water when the topsoil feels slightly dry- not completely dry or constantly wet.

  • How do I prune my bonsai?

    Prune regularly to maintain the shape and size of your bonsai. Use clean, sharp tools and trim unwanted shoots or branches to encourage healthy growth and styling

  • How often should I repot my bonsai?

    Most bonsai trees need repotting every 2–3 years to refresh the soil and prevent root binding. Younger trees may need it more frequently.
