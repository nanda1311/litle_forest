Life Inside the Glass

A Living Centerpiece

Our Founder Our Terrarium

Standing over six feet tall, this floor-to-ceiling glass terrarium is one of our signature installations — a striking example of how we bring nature into architectural spaces. Housed in a seamless glass enclosure, the piece centers around a dramatic niwaki-style pine, its cascading branches trained into elegant horizontal tiers reminiscent of traditional Japanese garden design.

The tree rises from a sculpted mound of dense, textured moss, accented with natural stone and delicate white blooms — a miniature landscape that feels both wild and meticulously composed. Soft overhead lighting built into the glass canopy highlights the sculptural form of the pine against a clean, minimalist backdrop.
