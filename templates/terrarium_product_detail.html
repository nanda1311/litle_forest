Care Guide & Ideal Setting

Light Prefers bright, indirect light. Avoid direct harsh sunlight, as it can overheat the terrarium and damage moisture-loving plants.
Watering Keep the soil slightly moist but not waterlogged. Mist occasionally if the terrarium is open. Closed terrariums require very little watering.
Temperature Thrives in 18°C–27°C (65°F–80°F). Keep away from extreme heat, cold drafts, and frost.
