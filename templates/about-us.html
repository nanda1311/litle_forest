About Litle Forest

Welcome to Litle Forest- an online marketplace dedicated to the art of bonsai. At Litle Forest, we aim to bring together those who appreciate nature and the skill involved in creating and caring for bonsai trees. Whether you're looking to buy a bonsai tree online or simply explore different bonsais, our platform connects you to artists and sellers who are deeply committed to their craft.

We understand that a bonsai is not just a plant, but the result of time, care, and technique. From seasoned artists to new hobbyists searching for the perfect bonsai tree in Bangalore, Litle Forest provides a space where people can discover, learn, and be a part of the growing bonsai community.

MISSION

To offer a reliable platform where people can explore, learn about, and buy bonsai trees online. We aim to help bonsai artists showcase their work while making it easier for customers to find and care for the perfect bonsai tree and beyond- with guidance, trust, and community at the core

VISION

To support a growing community that values the art of bonsai. We aim to create a space where artists, collectors, and nature lovers come together to share their passion, learn from one another, and keep the tradition of bonsai alive for future generations.

Meet Our Founder - Sagar Varma

Sagar Varma is a creative professional with a deep appreciation for nature and a special fondness for bonsai. With a successful career in the creative industry, Sagar has always been drawn to the balance, detail, and patience that bonsai represents. What began as a personal interest gradually grew into a meaningful pursuit- leading to the creation of Litle Forest.

Inspired by the dedication of bonsai artists and the calm presence these trees bring, Sagar envisioned a platform where bonsai could be more accessible and appreciated. Litle Forest was born out of this vision- a space where artists can share their work, and people can explore, learn, and buy bonsai trees online with ease and trust.

When he’s not working on creative projects, Sagar enjoys caring for his own bonsai collection and finding inspiration in the natural world. Through Litle Forest, he hopes to encourage others to experience the quiet joy and connection that bonsai brings into our lives
